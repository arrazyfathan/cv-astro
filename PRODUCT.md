# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers and engineering leaders recruiting for senior Android, Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose, and Flutter roles. They are scanning quickly for credible ownership, technical range, product judgment, and clear ways to review work or start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present Ar Razy Fathan Rabbani as a grounded mobile engineer through selected case studies and concise evidence of experience. The homepage should earn a closer look; the dedicated resume and generated PDF should support detailed evaluation and sharing.

## Brand Personality

Warm, mechanical, grounded. Confident without inflated claims, technical without becoming cold, and direct without feeling generic.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a long online CV, a generic equal-card portfolio grid, a hidden command-palette demo, or a template filled with abstract developer decoration. Avoid unverified metrics, vague self-promotion, excessive monospace copy, and ornamental motion.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the work and the decisions behind it.
- Make technical depth easy to scan, then reward a closer read.
- Separate the portfolio narrative from the complete printable resume.
- Use one warm, memorable signal color with disciplined restraint.
- Keep every primary action visible and usable from a keyboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with semantic landmarks, strong contrast, visible focus, useful image alternatives, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain readable from 320px through large desktop widths and in print.
